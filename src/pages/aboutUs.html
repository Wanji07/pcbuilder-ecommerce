Who we are

A computer parts store for people who build.

B.Y.T.E. started in a small Quezon City workspace with one idea: buying the right hardware should be as satisfying as using it. Today, we're a small, dedicated team helping thousands of Filipinos create their ideal setup, one carefully chosen component at a time.
