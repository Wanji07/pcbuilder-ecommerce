ABOUT B.Y.T.E.

Build Your Tech Essentials.

B.Y.T.E. (Build Your Tech Essentials) is a proposed Philippine-based technology retail company established in 2024. It specializes in computer components, accessories, and peripherals for students, PC builders, gamers, and computer enthusiasts. Through its B.Y.T.E. online platform, B.Y.T.E. aims to provide customers with an organized and convenient way to explore and purchase computer hardware.

Mission To provide accessible and reliable computer hardware through a simple and convenient online shopping experience.
Vision To become a trusted technology retailer for Filipino PC enthusiasts.
Address Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines
Contact support@byte.ph
